👤 About Me 个人简介

Kang Chen is a Professor in the School of Computer Science at Peking University and has been appointed as a Distinguished Professor under a national talent program. His research has long focused on operating systems. He has published more than 70 papers in recent years, including papers at OSDI and SOSP, the premier international conferences in computer systems, and has received an OSDI Best Paper Award. He has long served on the program committees of OSDI, SOSP, EuroSys, NSDI, ASPLOS, and ATC. Since 2024, he has served as an Associate Editor of ACM Transactions on Computer Systems (TOCS). His honors include the First Prize of the State Scientific and Technological Progress Award, the Second Prize of the State Technological Invention Award, and the First Prize of the Science and Technology Progress Award of the Chinese Institute of Electronics. His recent research focuses on ubiquitous operating systems. He co-founded the Sihope Ubiquitous Operating System open-source community, leads the development of Robonix, an operating system for embodied intelligence, and actively promotes its open-source ecosystem.

北京大学计算机学院教授，入选国家级特聘人才，长期从事操作系统研究。近年来发表论文70余篇，包括OSDI / SOSP等计算机系统领域顶级国际会议，获得过OSDI最佳论文奖。长期担任OSDI / SOSP / EuroSys / NSDI / ASPLOS / ATC程序委员会委员。2024年开始担任ACM Transactions on Computer Systems （TOCS）的副主编。曾获国家科技进步一等奖，国家技术发明二等奖、中国电子学会科技进步一等奖等。近年来关注泛在操作系统研究，联合发起成立矽望泛在操作系统开源社区，主持研发Robonix具身智能操作系统并积极推动相关开源生态建设。

Academic exchanges and collaborations on operating systems, storage systems, ubiquitous operating systems, and related topics are welcome.

欢迎就操作系统、存储系统、泛在操作系统及相关方向开展学术交流与合作。
